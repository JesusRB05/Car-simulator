# Code of conduct

This is a community project built by people with very different backgrounds:
engineers, sim racers, modders and students. Everyone is welcome as long as
they help keep the space friendly.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; reviews are about the code.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people whose first language is not English.
- Do not harass, discriminate, insult or publish other people's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of sexual or violent content.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where
people represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads and block people who
break these rules. To report a problem privately, open a draft security
advisory in the repository's **Security** tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
and mark it as a conduct report.
